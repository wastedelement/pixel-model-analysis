# External dependencies and assets

This archive does not bundle third-party libraries, pretrained weights, fonts,
or datasets. Keep their licenses and notices when obtaining or redistributing
them separately.

- PIXEL implementation: https://github.com/xplip/pixel . Its upstream license
  is Apache License 2.0: https://github.com/xplip/pixel/blob/main/LICENSE .
- Hugging Face Transformers: https://github.com/huggingface/transformers .
  Apache License 2.0.
- BERT-base pretrained assets: https://huggingface.co/bert-base-uncased .
  Refer to the model card and accompanying notices.
- PIXEL-base pretrained assets: https://huggingface.co/Team-PIXEL/pixel-base .
  Refer to the model card, renderer assets, and accompanying notices.
- SST-2/GLUE: https://huggingface.co/datasets/nyu-mll/glue .
- MultiNLI: https://cims.nyu.edu/~sbowman/multinli/ .

The PAWS-X- and Taxi1500-adapted checkpoints are additional experiment inputs,
not files in this release. Public upstream author/project names in these links
are attribution, not contact details for this submission.
